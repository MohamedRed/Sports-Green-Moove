# Accent Controls, 30 October 2026

## Failure And Correction

The original JVM measurement runs eighteen actual production-token pairs, with
eleven assertion failures: green text on five light neutral surfaces, white text
on four bright brand fills and white control icons on green/orange. A separate
earlier eighteen-case constructor error produced no accepted contrast measures;
its original XML/source are retained under `preparation/`.

The corrected role-specific foreground is dark on flat green/orange controls.
Semantic accent text is stronger green on light surfaces and retains brand green
on dark surfaces. The shared button, chip, tab, badge, avatar and navigation roles
use these colors; dark hero white is preserved. Fixed brand tokens and custom
control fills, neutral text values, dimensions, fonts, routes and business behavior
are unchanged. The default Material primary uses the stronger semantic green in
light mode, with white content, and brand green with dark content in dark mode;
its text actions therefore also use a readable foreground. The unused arbitrary
avatar fill parameter is removed; every current caller uses the fixed brand fill.

Twenty actual measurements now pass, including two added default Material primary
role pairs. Minimum normal-text ratio is 5.525185747428838; text on green is
7.38106479588164 and on orange is 6.35575453417362. Threshold comparisons are
unrounded, alpha is composited before luminance and the original eighteen case
names remain. Text uses 4.5:1 and control icons 3:1, following the
[W3C text](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html) and
[non-text contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html)
criteria. This does not establish complete WCAG conformance.
