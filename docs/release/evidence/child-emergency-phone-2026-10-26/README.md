# Child Contact With Location Denied

On 26 October, a disposable Google Pixel 9 Pro / Android 15 in BrowserStack App
Live signed into an isolated child account and retrieved Lina QA's saved contact.
Android Settings explicitly showed **Don't allow** selected for the app's location
permission. After returning from Settings/Home, the app again displayed Lina,
the fictitious contact and +12025550121. No permission was changed, no maps were
requested and no real customer data or payment was used.

The child login was provisioned by the fixture. The verified-parent account link,
free active ride and contact were prepared through authenticated local services
before this trial. This is not signup, parent-contact-editor UI, booking UI or
approval UI evidence. Earlier separate invitation and pairing trials stay dated
separately. Account UID and child-profile ID are distinct.

Five ordered independent checkpoints verify the current callable result and
unchanged records, claims and live data. Their completed field refers only to
that read-only observer. In particular, dialer-return means the app was reopened
after an attempted handoff, not that a dialer was observed. All observer
permission/dialer/UI proof flags remain false; manual UI review is separate.

The app's Open Phone button returned to Android Home. Independently tapping
Android's Phone icon also showed Home, not a dialer. Reopening our app retrieved
the same contact and sign-out returned to login. No call was placed, no dialer
handoff is accepted and no app crash or provider-policy cause is established.
During the Settings return, a temporary session spinner and Home were observed;
this does not prove an application process restart. No restriction was bypassed,
no alternate dialer installed and no subscription upgraded.
